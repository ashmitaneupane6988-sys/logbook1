Check the current ATO cents-per-km rate and its 5,000 km yearly cap on ato.gov.au, then update the rate above. Travel between home and a regular workplace is generally not claimable, but travel between clients usually is. Keep your own notes of what each trip was for. Which of the three types you can claim depends on your situation (for example, km that your employer or a client already reimburses can't be claimed again), so check with the ATO or your tax agent.

Add a trip

Leave the date or time blank and it records when you save. Type your odometer start and end and the km is worked out for you. If you forgot the start reading, enter the end reading and the km, and the start is worked out (end minus km). If you only have the start and km, the end is worked out. Calculated readings show a * in the log. For A to B to C to D, save each leg as its own trip. The second button copies the "To" client and the end odometer into the next leg.

Export & backup

Excel

Downloads a .csv that opens straight in Excel or Google Sheets. It has a summary and a month-by-month table at the top, then three separate sections with subtotals: km with client, km between clients, and travel between jobs.

Backup file

Download a backup file and keep it somewhere safe (on your phone you can save it to Files or Google Drive). To load it on another device or after clearing your browser, use Restore. Restoring merges with what is already there.

Your data is saved in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data it is lost, so export each month. On your phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option to open this like an app.
